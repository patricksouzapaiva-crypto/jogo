# Ep. 07 "A ronda" — prompts dos efeitos sonoros

Para usar em qualquer gerador de efeitos (ElevenLabs Sound Effects, Stable Audio, etc.). Os prompts estão em
inglês porque esses geradores entendem melhor.
- Gere **2 ou 3 versões de cada** e me mande todas. Pode mandar sem renomear: eu escuto e escolho.
- **Ambientes:** marque **loop** se o gerador tiver essa opção, com a maior duração permitida.
- **Efeitos:** use a duração indicada.
- Nada de música, vozes ou falas, a não ser onde o prompt pede.

## Ambientes (tocam por baixo da narração)
| Código | Duração | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **S01 — hospital à noite** | 30 s, loop | Empty old hospital corridor at night, low hum of fluorescent tube lights, distant air conditioning, a faint heart monitor beeping far away every few seconds, very quiet, realistic room tone, no voices, no music |
| **S02 — silêncio errado** | 30 s, loop | Unnaturally silent hospital room at night, only a very faint low room tone, no hum, no beeps, no traffic, oppressive stillness, no music |
| **S03 — colônia abandonada** | 30 s, loop | Abandoned decaying asylum corridor, cold wind whistling through broken windows, slow water drips, distant creaking metal, dusty empty space, no voices, no music |
| **S04 — apartamento de madrugada** | 30 s, loop | Small city apartment at 2 am, very quiet, distant traffic hum far away, a refrigerator buzzing softly, no voices, no music |

## A corrente (o som principal do episódio)
| Código | Duração | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **S05 — corrente longe** | 10 s | Heavy rusty iron chain slowly dragged across a tiled hospital floor far down a long empty corridor, link by link, echoing, distant and muffled, slow steady pace, no footsteps, no music |
| **S06 — corrente chegando** | 10 s | Heavy rusty iron chain slowly dragged across a tiled floor, getting closer, link by link, metallic scraping and clinking, slow heavy pace, ends stopping right outside a door, no footsteps, no music |
| **S07 — corrente parando** | 4 s | A heavy iron chain dragged a few last links across a tiled floor and then stopping completely, a final soft metallic settle, close perspective, no music |
| **S08 — corrente indo embora** | 10 s | Heavy iron chain slowly dragged away down a long tiled corridor, link by link, fading into the distance until silence, echo, no music |
| **S09 — corrente rápida** | 6 s | Heavy iron chains dragged fast across a stone floor, rushing closer through a dark corridor, frantic metallic rattling, getting very close, no voices, no music |

## A respiração e a porta
| Código | Duração | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **S10 — respiração atrás da porta** | 12 s | Slow, deep, wet breathing of something large heard from the other side of a closed wooden door, muffled, patient, listening, inhale and exhale every four seconds, no voice, no music |
| **S11 — respiração na nuca** | 6 s | Very close cold heavy breathing right next to the listener's ear, slow, wet, raspy, intimate and unsettling, no voice, no music |
| **S12 — maçaneta** | 3 s | Old metal door handle slowly turning halfway with a rusty creak, then stopping, door stays closed, quiet room, no music |
| **S13 — ferrolho** | 3 s | Old heavy iron door bolt slowly slid open with a rusty scrape, then a wooden door creaking open, no music |
| **S14 — porta fechando** | 3 s | Old wooden door closed quickly but quietly, a dull thud, then silence, no music |
