# Pedido de imagens para o ChatGPT — O Vigia, Ep. 02 "A Sonâmbula"

**Total: 25 imagens da história + 1 cenário do Vigia + 1 arte de abertura + 2 folhas de
personagem (só referência) = 29 pedidos.**

Por que 25 e não 55: cada imagem foi pensada para render **2 ou 3 enquadramentos** (plano aberto →
aproximação lenta até um detalhe). Assim a tela muda a cada ~6–10 s sem virar slide, e o vídeo de
~10 min fica com imagens "vivas" por mais tempo. O relógio 03:12 e o horário da câmera de segurança
eu desenho no vídeo — não precisam ser gerados.

---

## COMO USAR
1. Abra **uma conversa nova** no ChatGPT e cole o **BLOCO 1 (instruções gerais)**.
2. Peça as **folhas de personagem** (R1 e R2). Quando ficarem boas, diga: *"Use estas duas
   folhas como referência obrigatória de rosto, cabelo e roupa em todas as próximas imagens."*
   Se o ChatGPT perder a consistência mais tarde, **reenvie as duas folhas** junto do pedido.
3. Peça as imagens **uma por vez**, colando o texto de cada uma (são em inglês: o gerador
   entende melhor e erra menos).
4. **Formato:** todas na **horizontal, 1536×1024** (vídeo longo no YouTube é horizontal). Se
   preferir vídeo vertical, troque no BLOCO 1 "landscape 1536x1024" por "portrait 1024x1536".
5. Salve com o número da imagem (ex.: `I07.png`) e me envie em um **.zip** ou numa mensagem só
   com as imagens.

## BLOCO 1 — instruções gerais (colar primeiro)

```
You will generate a series of illustrations for a dark psychological horror story told in a YouTube video.
Rules for EVERY image in this conversation:
- Format: landscape 1536x1024. Keep the main subject inside the central 80% of the frame (the video will crop and slowly zoom).
- Style: dark cinematic horror illustration, semi-realistic digital painting, realistic human proportions, subtle film grain. Cold palette: deep teal, desaturated blue-green and near-black shadows, pale moonlight; warm light only when I ask. Consistent lighting and color between images, as if they were frames from the same film.
- Setting: a small, simple, slightly old Brazilian house at night (wooden double bed, bookshelf, curtains, narrow hallway, simple kitchen with an old refrigerator and a 4-burner gas stove).
- Horror is psychological: NO monsters, NO blood, NO gore, NO supernatural elements. Fear comes only from expressions, posture, stillness and darkness.
- NEVER write text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks in the image (clock screens must be blank).
- Keep the two characters IDENTICAL in every image (face, hair, body, clothes), following the character sheets.
Characters:
- HELENA: woman, about 30, long straight dark hair, pale skin, thin face, dark eyes; always wears a simple long white sleeveless nightgown at night.
- MARCOS: man, about 33, short dark-brown hair, short beard, average build; wears a plain grey t-shirt and dark sweatpants at night.
```

## IMAGENS DA HISTÓRIA

### Abertura (cold open) e o "olhar"
**I01 — Ela de pé ao lado da cama** *(a imagem mais importante do episódio; usada 3 vezes)*
```
Night, dark bedroom seen from Marcos's point of view lying on his side in bed (his shoulder and the pillow blurred in the foreground, bottom-left). HELENA stands perfectly still beside the bed in her white nightgown, arms hanging at her sides, looking straight down at him (toward the camera) with wide open, unblinking eyes and a blank face. Pale moonlight through the curtains falls on half of her face; the rest is in shadow. A small nightstand with a dark digital clock (blank screen) between them. Unsettling stillness.
```

**I02 — Close do rosto dela**
```
Extreme close-up of HELENA's face in the dark, lit only by cold moonlight from the side: eyes wide open and fixed, pupils large, lips slightly parted as if about to whisper, strands of dark hair over her cheek. No smile. Deep shadows. Intimate and disturbing.
```

**I03 — A casa**
```
Exterior, night, light rain: a small, simple one-story Brazilian house at the end of a quiet dead-end street, one warm-lit bedroom window, wet asphalt reflecting a single street lamp, dark trees around. Calm but lonely.
```

**I04 — Dobrando roupas de madrugada**
```
Night, bedroom lit by a weak bedside lamp: HELENA sits on the edge of the bed in her nightgown folding clothes that are already folded, eyes half open and unfocused, movements calm. MARCOS asleep in the background, out of focus. Quiet and slightly odd, not scary yet.
```

**I05 — No jardim, debaixo de chuva**
```
Night, small backyard garden in the rain: HELENA in her soaked white nightgown calmly watering potted plants with a watering can, eyes half closed, hair wet, as if it were a sunny afternoon. Light from the kitchen door behind her. Strange, melancholic.
```

**I06 — Na geladeira (de costas)** *(usada no Ato 1 e na "noite que mudou tudo")*

```
Night, dark small kitchen: HELENA stands with her back to the camera in front of an old open refrigerator; the fridge light is the only light source, casting her long silhouette across the floor and outlining her nightgown and hair. She is completely still. Seen from the dark hallway doorway.
```

**I07 — Ela vira a cabeça e sorri**
```
Night, kitchen, lit from below and the side by the open refrigerator: HELENA looks back over her shoulder toward the camera, head turned slowly, with a faint closed-lip smile that does NOT reach her eyes; eyes wide and empty. Cold fridge light on her face, everything else dark. Deeply unsettling.
```

**I08 — Levando ela de volta pela mão**
```
Night, narrow dark hallway: MARCOS gently leads HELENA by the hand back toward the bedroom; she walks with eyes half open, unaware; he looks tired and protective. Faint light from the kitchen at the end of the hallway behind them.
```

**I09 — Café da manhã** *(único momento quente)*
```
Morning, small kitchen with warm sunlight through the window: MARCOS and HELENA at the table with coffee, both laughing, relaxed and in love. Cozy and warm colors. (This is the only warm image of the story.)
```
