# Pedido de imagens — Ep. 03: a criatura escondida que se aproxima

A sequência H1→H8 é o coração do episódio: **a mesma criatura escondida em todas as imagens, cada
vez mais perto do Rafael**. Ela precisa estar **quase invisível** (do tom da sombra), mas **presente
de verdade** na imagem — no final eu clareio a região e ela aparece.

## Bloco base (colar primeiro)
```
I will ask for a series of illustrations for a psychological horror story. Rules for EVERY image:
- Landscape 1536x1024, dark cinematic horror, semi-realistic digital painting, cold desaturated teal and near-black shadows, realistic proportions, subtle film grain, no text, no watermark.
- Main character RAFAEL: man, 34, short messy dark hair, light stubble, tired eyes, grey hoodie and dark sweatpants, barefoot at home. Keep him identical in every image.
- Setting: a small old Brazilian apartment at night with a long narrow hallway, wooden floor, old doors, few lamps.
- THE HIDDEN FIGURE: in every image there is a very tall, unnaturally thin pale-grey humanoid standing perfectly still, long arms hanging close to the body, smooth expressionless face, dark eyes, head slightly tilted. It must be HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT: placed in the darkest shadow of the scene, almost the same tone as the shadow, low contrast, partly covered by a door frame or darkness, so that a casual viewer does NOT notice it, but a careful viewer can find it. Never in the center, never lit, never sharp. It is always facing Rafael.
- Rafael never looks at the figure.
```

## A sequência (a distância diminui a cada imagem)
**H1 — A rua (muito longe)**
```
Night, a quiet residential street in the rain. Rafael walks toward the entrance of an old apartment building in the foreground, carrying grocery bags, seen from behind. Far away at the end of the street, between two trees beyond the last street lamp, the hidden figure stands in the dark, tiny and almost invisible.
```
**H2 — A sala e a janela (reflexo distante)**
```
Night, small living room, only the TV light. Rafael on the couch looking at his phone, in the foreground. Behind him, a big dark window reflecting the room; deep inside the reflection, in the far dark corner of the reflected room, the hidden figure stands, barely visible, like a faint shape in the glass.
```
**H3 — O fim do corredor (longe)**
```
Night, the long narrow hallway seen from one end. Rafael in the foreground, walking away from the camera toward the bedroom with a glass of water. At the very far end of the hallway, inside the black opening of a half-open door, the hidden figure stands in the darkness, only a faint grey outline of its head and shoulder.
```
**H4 — A cozinha (no batente)**
```
Night, small kitchen, light only from the open fridge. Rafael bends to look inside the fridge. In the dark doorway behind him, about 4 meters away, half of the hidden figure is visible: one side of its body and part of its smooth face behind the door frame, in deep shadow.
```
**H5 — O quarto (no canto)**
```
Night, bedroom lit only by the phone screen. Rafael lies in bed on his side, facing away from the room, looking at his phone. In the darkest corner of the room, beside the wardrobe, about 3 meters from the bed, the hidden figure stands motionless, its grey shape blending with the wall shadow.
```
**H6 — O banheiro (atrás da cortina)**
```
Night, small bathroom, a weak yellow bulb. Rafael brushes his teeth looking down at the sink, the mirror in front of him. Behind him, through the semi-transparent shower curtain, 2 meters away, the faint dark silhouette of the hidden figure stands very still, only noticeable as a slightly darker tall shape.
```
**H7 — O corredor de novo (no meio)**
```
Night, the same long hallway as before, seen from the same end. Rafael walks away from the camera in the hallway, almost at the bedroom door. In the middle of the hallway, about 2 meters behind him, the hidden figure stands in the gap of darkness between two lamps, nearly black, only its pale long fingers and the edge of its face barely catching light.
```
**H8 — A câmera frontal (um passo)**
```
Phone selfie-camera view at night: Rafael's frightened face in the foreground, close, lit by the phone screen, looking at the screen, the dark hallway behind him. Directly behind his shoulder, one step away, in the dark, the hidden figure's smooth grey face is just visible in the noise of the dark background, eyes level with his head.
```
**H9 — O celular no chão (final, sem criatura)**
```
Night, the hallway floor seen from a phone lying on the wooden floor, camera pointing up at an angle: the ceiling, a hanging bulb swinging slightly, an open door. Rafael's slippers on the floor. Nobody in the frame. Cold, empty, silent.
```
